How Sculpt & Surge Works

Stage 1 – Sculpt

The first phase focuses on aggressively reducing body fat while preserving strength and muscle.

  • Target: Sub 12% body fat (men)
  • Fat loss rate: 0.75kg – 1.5kg per week
  • Maintain performance and muscle mass

Stage 2 – Stabilise

After reaching a lean baseline we hold maintenance calories for approximately 4 weeks.

  • Restore hormones and hunger signals
  • Refill glycogen stores
  • Begin reverse dieting

Stage 3 – Surge

The final stage branches into two athletic pathways.

  • Lean muscle growth
  • Speed, agility and power training
  • Performance based programming
