About Me

Hi, I’m Jayden. As someone that started by diving head first into the gym with a few mates, I’ve gone through the confusion between the real and the myth with nutrition and training.

Over the years I’ve shifted from sport and mentality — incorporating fitness into a lifestyle rather than a chore. Using the gym to supplement the weaknesses I’ve felt in sport and most importantly; keeping it bloody damn FUN.

It's impossible to stay committed to something you hate. Fitness should empower you — not feel like punishment.

I want to show how sustainable and enjoyable getting fit can be. With healthy adjustments, you CAN keep the foods you love while still achieving your goals.

Pique your interest? Come connect through socials and get a slice of life with me. Everyone is welcome here.
