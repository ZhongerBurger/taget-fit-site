FAQ

Common questions

How do I start?

DM “SCULPT” on Instagram or send an email. We’ll chat about your goal, training history and sport, then map your starting stage.

Do I have to give up foods I love?

No. The approach is built on sustainable adjustments, not a restrictive diet.

Is Sculpt & Surge only for lifters?

No. It suits anyone playing sport or wanting to move better, including volleyball, bouldering, running and basketball.

How fast will I see progress?

Sculpt is a structured fat loss phase with a rate of 0.75–1.5kg per week, then stabilise and surge. This is a multi-month system, not a 4-week fix.
